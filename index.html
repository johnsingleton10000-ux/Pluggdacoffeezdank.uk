Founder Story

DaCoFFeezDank was built through cerebral palsy, pain, resilience and creativity. This is not corporate wellness. This is a UK underground movement turning struggle into colour, community, coffee, hemp-derived botanical extracts and futuristic street art.


The mission is simple: build a space where people feel seen, welcomed and part of something different — the cannabinoid tea revolution.
